MinimaDock {VERSION}
====================

A glass dock that replaces the Windows 11 taskbar: your apps, a launcher
(click the dock's left button or tap the Windows key), media controls, quick
settings, calendar, tray and prayer times.

Requirements: Windows 11, 64-bit. No administrator rights needed.


Install
-------
1. Unzip the whole folder (right-click the zip > Extract All).
2. Double-click "Install MinimaDock.cmd".

MinimaDock starts right away and every time you sign in. Your Windows
taskbar is only hidden, never removed.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" > "Run anyway".
MinimaDock is not code-signed. If Smart App Control blocks it, the installer
stops and nothing is changed.


Using it
--------
- Hover the dock to expand it. Right-click empty dock space for
  "MinimaDock settings..." (theme, colours, full-width bar, prayer times,
  updates).
- Prayer times: add your masjid's website in Customize > Prayer times.
  Until then no prayer times are shown.
- Ctrl+Alt+Backspace brings back the Windows taskbar until you sign in again.


Uninstall
---------
Double-click "Uninstall MinimaDock.cmd", or use
Settings > Apps > Installed apps > MinimaDock > Uninstall.
The Windows taskbar comes straight back.
